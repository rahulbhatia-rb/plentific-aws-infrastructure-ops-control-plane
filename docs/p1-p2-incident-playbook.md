# P1/P2 Incident Playbook

1. Confirm customer impact and severity.
2. Assign incident commander.
3. Check recent deployments and infrastructure changes.
4. Classify AWS/Linux/network/deploy/dependency/application.
5. Preserve evidence before random fixes.
6. Prefer reversible mitigation: rollback, failover, controlled restart, infrastructure recovery.
7. Validate the customer path, not just host health.
8. Capture timeline and decisions.
9. Convert root cause into Terraform, Ansible, monitoring, or runbook improvements.
